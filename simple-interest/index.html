How simple interest is calculated

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal amount. Unlike compound interest, previously earned interest is not added to the principal when calculating future interest.

Simple interest formula

Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time ÷ 100

The rate is expressed as an annual percentage and the time is expressed in years.

Total amount

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

0% interest

If the interest rate is 0%, no interest is earned or charged, so the total amount remains equal to the principal.

Simple interest vs compound interest

With simple interest, interest is calculated only on the original principal. With compound interest, previously accumulated interest can also contribute to future interest calculations.

This makes simple interest calculations straightforward and useful for situations where interest does not compound.
