Frequently Asked Questions

What can I convert with this unit converter?

You can convert common length, weight and temperature units. Supported units include meters, kilometers, miles, feet, inches, kilograms, pounds, ounces, Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

How do I convert one unit to another?

Select the category you need, enter your value, choose the original unit under From, choose the target unit under To, and click Convert.

How do I convert kilometers to miles?

Select Length, enter the number of kilometers, select Kilometers as the original unit and Miles as the target unit, then click Convert.

How do I convert kilograms to pounds?

Select Weight, choose Kilograms under From and Pounds under To, enter your value and click Convert.

Can I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Yes. Select Temperature, choose Celsius as the original unit and Fahrenheit as the target unit. The converter automatically applies the appropriate temperature conversion.

Does the converter work with decimal values?

Yes. You can enter decimal values such as 2.5, 10.75 or 0.125 for more precise conversions.

Does this unit converter work on mobile?

Yes. The calculator is designed to work on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.
