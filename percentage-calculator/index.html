How to calculate a percentage

A percentage represents a number as a part of 100. The word "percent" means "per hundred."

Finding a percentage of a number

To find X% of Y, multiply Y by X and divide the result by 100.

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

For example, 20% of 150 is 30.

Finding what percentage one number is of another

Divide the first number by the total number and multiply by 100.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

For example, 30 is 20% of 150.

Calculating percentage change

Percentage change compares an original value with a new value.

Percentage Change = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

A positive result represents an increase, while a negative result represents a decrease.

Percentage examples

What is 15% of 200?

15 ÷ 100 × 200 = 30

50 is what percentage of 200?

50 ÷ 200 × 100 = 25%

A price increases from 100 to 120. What is the percentage increase?

(120 − 100) ÷ 100 × 100 = 20%
