Convert text to uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case and alternating case instantly.

What is a case converter?

A case converter changes the capitalization of text without requiring you to manually retype it. This can be useful when editing documents, preparing text for websites, formatting headings or cleaning up copied text.

UtilityTools lets you quickly convert text into several common formats, including uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case and alternating case.

Available text cases

Uppercase: Converts every letter to capital letters.

Lowercase: Converts every letter to lowercase.

Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of words.

Sentence Case: Capitalizes the beginning of sentences.

Alternating Case: Alternates uppercase and lowercase letters throughout the text.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a case converter?

A case converter is a tool that changes the capitalization of existing text. It can convert text between uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case and other capitalization formats.

How do I convert text to uppercase?

Paste or type your text into the text box and click the UPPERCASE button. All letters will be converted to capital letters.

How do I convert text to lowercase?

Enter your text and click the lowercase button. The letters will be converted to lowercase while the rest of the text remains in place.

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of words. It is commonly used for headings, titles and other text where words are presented with initial capital letters.

What is Sentence Case?

Sentence case generally uses a capital letter at the beginning of a sentence while converting other letters to lowercase.

Can I copy the converted text?

Yes. After converting your text, click Copy Text to copy the result to your clipboard.

Is my text uploaded or stored?

The case conversion is performed directly in your browser using JavaScript. The tool does not need to send your text to a server to perform the conversion.
