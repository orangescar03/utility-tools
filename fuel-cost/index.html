How the fuel cost calculator works

Enter the distance of your trip, your vehicle's average mileage and the current fuel price per litre. The calculator estimates the amount of fuel required and the approximate cost of the trip.

Fuel required is calculated by dividing the distance by the vehicle's mileage. The estimated fuel cost is then calculated by multiplying the fuel required by the price per litre.

Example fuel cost calculation

If you travel 300 km in a vehicle that averages 15 km per litre, the trip would require approximately 20 litres of fuel. At ₹100 per litre, the estimated fuel cost would be ₹2,000.

What affects actual fuel consumption?

Actual fuel consumption can vary because of traffic, driving speed, road conditions, vehicle load, tyre pressure, weather and driving habits. This calculator provides an estimate based on the mileage you enter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?

Enter your trip distance, vehicle mileage and fuel price. The calculator divides the distance by mileage to estimate fuel required, then multiplies that amount by the fuel price.

How much fuel will my trip require?

Estimated fuel required is calculated by dividing the trip distance by your vehicle's mileage. For example, 450 km at 15 km/l requires approximately 30 litres of fuel.

What does km/l mean?

km/l means kilometres per litre. It describes how many kilometres a vehicle can travel using one litre of fuel. A higher km/l figure generally means better fuel efficiency.

Can I use this calculator for a round trip?

Yes. Enter the total distance for the complete journey. For a round trip, you can multiply the one-way distance by two before entering it into the calculator.

Does the calculator include tolls or other travel costs?

No. The calculator estimates fuel cost only. Tolls, parking, maintenance and other travel expenses are not included.

Why can my actual fuel cost be different?

Your actual fuel consumption may differ from the stated mileage because of traffic, speed, road conditions, vehicle load, weather and driving style. Changes in fuel prices will also affect the final cost.
