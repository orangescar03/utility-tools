How the fuel cost calculator works

Enter the distance you plan to travel, your vehicle's mileage and the current fuel price. The calculator estimates how many litres of fuel the trip will require and the approximate fuel cost.

For example, a 300 km trip in a vehicle that gives 15 km per litre requires approximately 20 litres of fuel.
