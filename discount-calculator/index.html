How to calculate a discount

A discount reduces the original price of an item by a certain percentage. To calculate the discount amount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100.

Discount Amount = Original Price × (Discount % ÷ 100)

Then subtract the discount amount from the original price to find the final sale price.

Sale Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Discount examples

20% off ₹1,000

Discount = ₹1,000 × 20% = ₹200. The final sale price is ₹800.

30% off ₹2,500

Discount = ₹2,500 × 30% = ₹750. The final sale price is ₹1,750.

15% off ₹800

Discount = ₹800 × 15% = ₹120. The final sale price is ₹680.
