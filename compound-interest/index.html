How compound interest works

Compound interest allows interest to be added to the existing balance, so future interest can be earned on both the original amount and previously accumulated interest.

This calculator uses the standard compound-interest formula and lets you choose how frequently the interest is compounded.

Results are estimates and assume the interest rate remains constant throughout the entire period.
