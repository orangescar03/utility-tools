How EMI is calculated

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed monthly amount paid toward a loan when using a standard amortizing loan calculation.

The calculation uses the loan amount, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments. The results are estimates and actual loan payments may differ depending on lender fees, taxes, insurance or other charges.
