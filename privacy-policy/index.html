Privacy Policy

This page explains how information may be handled when you use Utility Tools.

Information we collect

Utility Tools is designed so that the calculators and utilities can generally be used without creating an account or providing personal information.

Information may nevertheless be collected automatically by services used to operate, secure, measure, or improve the website. This may include technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, referring pages, and usage information.

How information is used

Information that is collected may be used to operate and maintain the website, understand how visitors use the tools, improve performance and usability, prevent abuse, and maintain website security.

Cookies and similar technologies

Utility Tools or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies. These technologies can help with website functionality, analytics, security, advertising, or remembering preferences.

Third-party services

The website may use third-party services for purposes such as hosting, analytics, security, or advertising. These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Calculator information

The calculators on Utility Tools are intended to perform calculations directly in your browser. Information entered into a calculator is not intentionally sent to us merely because you use the calculator.

Children's privacy

Utility Tools is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, its features, or the services used to operate it change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how the website handles information, please use the Contact page.
