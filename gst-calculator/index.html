How the GST calculator works

When adding GST, the calculator calculates the GST amount from the pre-GST price and adds it to find the final price. When removing GST, it calculates the GST component contained within a GST-inclusive price and returns the original price before GST.

For example, adding 18% GST to ₹1,000 gives a GST amount of ₹180 and a total price of ₹1,180.
