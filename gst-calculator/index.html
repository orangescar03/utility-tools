How the GST calculator works

GST, or Goods and Services Tax, is a tax applied to the supply of goods and services in India. This calculator can calculate GST both by adding GST to a price and by removing GST from a GST-inclusive price.

Adding GST

When you have a price before GST, the GST amount is calculated using the GST rate and then added to the original price.

GST Amount = Price × (GST Rate ÷ 100)
Total Price = Price + GST Amount

Removing GST

When the amount already includes GST, the pre-GST price can be calculated by dividing the total by one plus the GST rate.

Price Before GST = Inclusive Price ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

GST examples

18% GST on ₹1,000

GST = ₹1,000 × 18% = ₹180. The total price including GST is ₹1,180.

5% GST on ₹2,000

GST = ₹2,000 × 5% = ₹100. The total price including GST is ₹2,100.

Removing 18% GST from ₹1,180

The price before GST is ₹1,000 and the GST component is ₹180.
