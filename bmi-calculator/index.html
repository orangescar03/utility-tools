What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a calculation based on a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening measure for adults. BMI does not directly measure body fat and should not be used as a diagnosis of health.

For adults, a BMI below 18.5 is generally classified as underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 as healthy weight, 25.0 to 29.9 as overweight, and 30.0 or higher as obesity.
