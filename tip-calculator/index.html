How the tip calculator works

Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator shows the tip amount, total bill and the amount each person should pay.

For example, a 15% tip on a ₹2,000 bill is ₹300, making the total bill ₹2,300. If two people split it equally, each person pays ₹1,150.
