Bill Splitter

Split a restaurant bill between people and calculate the tip, total bill, and amount each person should pay.

Your result Enter your bill amount above.

How the bill splitter works

Enter the total bill, choose the tip percentage, and enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator adds the tip to the bill and divides the total equally between everyone.

Calculate the tip

Tip = Bill Amount × (Tip Percentage ÷ 100)

Calculate the total bill

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip

Split the bill

Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Bill splitting examples

₹2,000 bill with a 15% tip for 2 people

The tip is ₹300, making the total bill ₹2,300. Each person pays ₹1,150.

₹3,000 bill with a 10% tip for 3 people

The tip is ₹300, making the total bill ₹3,300. Each person pays ₹1,100.

₹1,500 bill with no tip for 3 people

The total remains ₹1,500, so each person pays ₹500.
