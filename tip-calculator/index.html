How the bill splitter works

Enter the total bill amount, choose a tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator shows the tip, total bill and the amount each person should pay.

For example, a ₹2,000 bill with a 15% tip adds ₹300 in tip, making the total ₹2,300. If two people split the bill equally, each person pays ₹1,150.

How is the bill split?

The calculator first calculates the tip based on the bill amount. It then adds the tip to the original bill to find the total amount. Finally, the total is divided equally by the number of people.

Can the bill be split without a tip?

Yes. Enter 0 as the tip percentage. The calculator will divide the original bill equally between the number of people you enter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I split a restaurant bill?

Enter the total bill amount, enter the desired tip percentage and enter the number of people sharing the bill. The calculator will show the total bill and the amount each person should pay.

How is the tip calculated?

The tip is calculated by multiplying the bill amount by the tip percentage divided by 100.

Can I split the bill without adding a tip?

Yes. Enter 0% for the tip and the calculator will divide the original bill equally between the people.

Can I split a bill between more than two people?

Yes. Enter the total number of people sharing the bill. The calculator divides the final bill equally between them.

Does everyone pay the same amount?

The calculator assumes an equal split, so everyone pays the same amount. It does not currently support different amounts for individual people.

Can I use this for a restaurant or group meal?

Yes. You can use the bill splitter for restaurants, cafes, group meals, travel expenses or any other bill that needs to be divided equally.
