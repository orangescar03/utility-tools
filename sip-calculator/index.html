How the SIP calculator works

Enter your monthly investment, expected annual rate of return and investment duration. The calculator estimates the amount you could accumulate and separates your total contributions from the estimated investment returns.

The calculation assumes a constant rate of return compounded monthly. Actual mutual fund returns fluctuate and are not guaranteed.

This calculator is for estimation and educational purposes only. It is not investment advice and does not guarantee future returns.
